# Bright Coffee Shop – Sales Analysis

## 1. Introduction

For this case study, I will be analysing the sales data provided for Bright Coffee Shop. The aim is to understand the business's sales performance and identify areas where improvements can be made.

I will be using data analytics to investigate which products generate the most revenue, what times of the day have the highest sales and how different products perform over time.

Through this project, I want to turn the raw sales data into useful information that can help the business make better decisions and improve its overall performance.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main things I want to find out through this analysis are:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the most and the least?
- What time of the day does the coffee shop receive the most sales?
- How do sales differ across products and time intervals?
- What can the business do to improve its sales performance?

## 3. Tools Used

These are the tools I will be using throughout the project:

- **Miro:** To plan the project, create my data flow diagram and map out the different project phases.
- **Databricks:** To upload, clean and process the sales data.
- **SQL:** To query the data, perform calculations and identify sales trends.
- **Microsoft Excel:** To analyse the processed data using pivot tables and charts.
- **GitHub:** To store my project files, document my progress and submit my completed work.

## 4. Project Approach

I have divided the case study into four main phases:

### Phase 1: Planning & Architecture

I started by planning how I would approach the case study using Miro. This includes creating a Data Flow and Architecture Diagram to show how the data will move from its original source through processing and analysis.

I will also use a Gantt chart to outline the different project phases and activities.

### Phase 2: Data Processing

For this phase, I will:

- Convert the original Excel dataset into CSV format.
- Upload the CSV file into Databricks.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Correct the unit price format where necessary.
- Create 30-minute time intervals for transactions.
- Calculate the total amount for each transaction using the unit price and quantity sold.

### Phase 3: Data Analysis

Once the data has been processed, I will use SQL and Excel to investigate:

- Total revenue generated by each product type.
- The busiest sales periods.
- The total quantity of products sold.
- Best-selling and underperforming products.
- Sales trends across different products and time intervals.

I will use pivot tables and charts to make the findings easier to understand.

### Phase 4: Presentation & Recommendations

In the final phase, I will put together a presentation highlighting the key findings from my analysis.

Based on the results, I will provide recommendations such as running promotions during quieter periods, maintaining stock of popular products and finding ways to improve the performance of slower-selling items.

## 5. Calculations

The following calculations will form part of my analysis:

**Total Amount**

`unit_price × transaction_qty`

This will help me calculate the total value of each transaction.

**Total Revenue**

`SUM(total_amount)`

This will show how much revenue was generated from sales.

**Total Units Sold**

`SUM(transaction_qty)`

This will help me determine the total number of items sold.

**Transaction Time Intervals**

I will group transactions into 30-minute intervals to identify which periods generate the most sales.

## 6. Expected Outcomes

By the end of this case study, I hope to have a better understanding of Bright Coffee Shop's sales performance.

I want to identify its strongest-performing products, understand customer purchasing patterns throughout the day and highlight opportunities that could help increase revenue.

The findings will also help me develop practical recommendations based on the actual sales data rather than assumptions.
